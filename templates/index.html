Welcome

GreenField College of Engineering is committed to nurturing innovation, technical excellence, and creative thinking among students. Through Technova 2k26, our annual technical and cultural fest, we bring together bright minds to showcase their skills across coding, design, presentation, and creative arts.

This year's fest features a diverse lineup — from PowerPoint Presentations and Debugging challenges to Quiz competitions, Photography, Ad-Zap, and even a fun Cooking without Fire event — designed to test both technical and creative talent.

Join us on 28th March 2026 at the GreenField Auditorium for a day full of competition, collaboration, and celebration of innovation.
